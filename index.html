About: Pedro Gonzalez

A passionate STEM advocate and eTIPS Fellow. He served as the College of Natural Sciences Director for Associated Students Incorporated (ASI) and as a Senior Science Buddy, helping CSUSB STEM students transition, get involved, and access resources.

Pedro partook in the ACTION AI summer research program at UC Santa Barbara last summer. He is the CSE Club Outreach Officer and actively involved in both the Cyber Intelligence & Security Organization (CISO) and the Society for Advancement of Chicanos and Native Americans in Science (SACNAS). Pedro participated in Caltech Up Close 2021, a prestigious program for students from historically underserved STEM backgrounds, gaining valuable exposure to academic opportunities. He is a Hispanic Scholarship Fund finalist and Hispanic Association of Colleges and Universities (HACU) 2024 Scholarship recipient, recognizing his academic excellence and leadership within the Hispanic community. Pedro also explored entrepreneurship through the Upstarters Discovery Entrepreneurship Camp. Web development, cybersecurity, and AI are areas of his technical expertise, supported by two cybersecurity certificates and a micro-credential in the Academic Applications of AI.

Pedro is a continuous learner and serves his community in and out of the classroom.
